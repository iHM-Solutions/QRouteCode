description=Converts vector lines and polylines into scannable Google Maps routing QR codes for mobile navigation.
about=This plugin automatically extracts the explicit coordinate vertices of a selected polyline layer, shifts them to WGS 84, and encodes them directly into an URL-safe Google Maps Directions API schema. The routing coordinates are dynamically translated into a high-density, dark blue QR code preview frame inside QGIS. External dependency: Contains an embedded 'qrcode' source vendor library.
